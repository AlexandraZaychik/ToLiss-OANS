Third-party components in this download
=======================================

1) B612 Mono font
   Copyright 2012 The B612 Project Authors (https://github.com/polarsys/b612)
   Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
   Included as a pre-rendered bitmap atlas:
     Resources/plugins/FlyWithLua/Scripts/A350_BTV_data/font_b612_px2.bin
   (B612 Mono Bold and Regular, rendered with FreeType; the Regular sizes slightly emboldened.)
   Full license text: Resources/plugins/FlyWithLua/Scripts/A350_BTV_data/font_b612_OFL.txt
   PolarSys, PolarSys B612 and B612 are trademarks of The Eclipse Foundation.

2) Synthetic voices (A350_BTV_data/sounds/voice1, voice2, voice3, and rops/keep_max_reverse.wav)
   Generated with the Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro,
   Apache License 2.0), run with sherpa-onnx (https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx, Apache License 2.0),
   then filtered to sound like a cockpit speaker.

3) OpenStreetMap data (not included - downloaded at runtime)
   Building outlines are downloaded from the public Overpass API servers when you visit an airport and
   cached in A350_BTV_data/osm2_<ICAO>.json.
   Data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL):
   https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

4) X-Plane airport data (not included - read at runtime)
   Runways, taxiways and exits are read from the apt.dat files of your own X-Plane installation.
