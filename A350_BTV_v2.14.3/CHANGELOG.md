# Changelog

## 2.14.3 – first public release

- **OANS:** airport map on the 3D-cockpit ND, with:
  - range 0.2 to 5 NM
  - ARC / ROSE / PLAN / NAV views
  - MAP DATA, ARPT SEL, LANDING and STATUS tabs
  - crosses and flags
  - OpenStreetMap buildings
  - traffic and RWY AHEAD
- **BTV:**
  - exit or stop-distance selection, ARM / DISARM
  - braking to 10 kt at the exit
  - BRK BTV in the FMA (cyan when armed, green when braking)
  - disarm on go-around
  - disconnect with the brake pedals or with X-Plane's brake commands (key / joystick button), so BTV also works without rudder pedals
- **ROW / ROP:**
  - runway condition from the LANDING tab
  - DRY / WET stop lines
  - PFD messages and aural alerts (REAL recording or three synthetic voices)
- **AUTO BRK OFF:** MASTER CAUT light and chime of the aircraft when the crew disconnects BTV.
- **Settings window:** options that work like the real A350 are marked (REAL).
